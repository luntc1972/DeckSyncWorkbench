### Written reason: Hexproof (status) ships singular-only

Every other effect above ships as a genuine singular/plural pair. Hexproof-status is the one
exception, and it is a *rejected-plural*, not an *inapplicable-plural*: `have hexproof` was
measured (see Rejected candidates below) and found to carry four confirmed false positives — real
hexproof-*removal*-enabler cards (Arcane Lighthouse, Detection Tower, Glaring Spotlight, Kaya, Bane
of the Dead), all matching via the negated construction "can't have hexproof" / "didn't have
hexproof", not via a genuine grant. No positive (non-negation-gate) substring narrower than bare
`have hexproof` was found that keeps the real plural grants (e.g. "Other Dinosaurs you control get
+1/+1 and have hexproof") while excluding the negated ones without also excluding legitimate
grants that do not read "creatures you control have hexproof" verbatim. Per this plan's own
guidance, adding a negation gate is a bigger correctness risk than shipping fewer needles, so
Hexproof-status ships singular-only this pass; `Cryptothrall`-style plural Hexproof-status grants
are a documented, accepted false negative, not a silent gap — Criterion 3 is unaffected because
none of the five ROADMAP-named misses depends on it.

### Written reason: Regenerate and Ward are subject-form `none`

Regenerate and Ward do not inflect by subject number the way "gain(s)" does — "Regenerate target
creature" and "Ward—Pay 2 life." are the same fixed ability text whether printed on the card
itself or granted to other permanents via a quoted static ability (Hexing Squelcher's own line and
its granted "have \"Ward—Pay 2 life.\"" clause both contain the identical `ward—pay` substring).
There is no plural counterpart to pair against, so both are recorded with subject form `none` per
this plan's own carve-out for effects with no grammatically varying subject, not exempted by a
name-based exception list.

## How to Reproduce

Ratified-set derivation (produces the table above):

```
python3 scripts/protection-vocab/derive-vocabulary.py --needle "gains hexproof" --needle "gain hexproof" --needle "gains indestructible" --needle "gain indestructible" --needle "gain protection from" --needle "gains protection from" --needle "phases out" --needle "phase out" --needle "has hexproof" --needle "has shroud" --needle "have shroud" --needle "gains shroud" --needle "gain shroud" --needle "regenerate target" --needle "regenerate this creature" --needle "ward—pay" --out docs/research/protection-vocabulary-corpus-2026-09.md
```

Rejected-candidate evidence derivation (produces the counts cited in "Rejected candidates" above):

```
python3 scripts/protection-vocab/derive-vocabulary.py --needle "have hexproof" --needle "shroud" --needle "regenerate" --needle "can't be regenerated" --needle "cannot be regenerated" --needle "be regenerated" --needle "ward-pay" --out /tmp/rejected-candidates.md
```

The archive is cached under `_protection-vocab-research/`, named from the descriptor's
`updated_at` stamp; re-running with the same cached archive present makes zero network
requests and reproduces byte-identical counts (the per-needle sample is the
alphabetically-first 10 matching names, independent of archive traversal order).
